## Implementation decisions

- Signed-out operation remains outside this milestone. The cost is that unattended work requires signed-in accounts.
- The approved roadmap and implementation request supplied authorization to proceed without repeated product approval questions. Routine choices remain reviewable in Git.
- Transport remains append-only and destructive maintenance belongs to the verified repository owner. The cost is that cleanup must be performed on the Backup Server.
- Development touched no installed setup, production tasks, real footage or production repositories. No main merge or public release was performed. Real-machine acceptance remains outstanding.
- Recovery uses native dumps, controlled extraction and independent per-file SHA-256 verification rather than applying historical Windows ACLs. This protects the current account during staging, at the cost of extra reads, processes and temporary space.
- Historical originals without positive Primary completion evidence remain explicitly unknown. They are still recoverable and retention-protected; historical media completion enrichment is deferred.
